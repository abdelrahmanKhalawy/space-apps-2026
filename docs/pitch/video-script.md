# Demo video script

Draft for the team (Nada and Ammar own the final cut). Written to fit **30 seconds**: about 75 spoken words at a calm pace. The 60-second live pitch is further down. Check the official 2026 Project Submission Guide for the real length limit before filming.

## 30-second version

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Black. White text: **"Day 143. Mars. 20 minutes to Earth."** Then the Status screen, all calm. | "Day 143 on the way to Mars. A message to Earth takes twenty minutes each way." |
| 5–11 s | Hand on a phone: fingertip on the camera. The pulse number appears and the body lights up. | "AstroPulse reads your pulse with the phone's camera. No extra hardware." |
| 11–19 s | **Replay** tab. Heart-rate row stays green, a flag lands on day 11. | "Fixed ranges say your heart rate is fine. AstroPulse compares you with *your own* normal, and flags the drift days earlier." |
| 19–25 s | Decision screen goes red: "40 min round trip, act now." | "When it turns red, it tells you what to do right now, and how long Earth needs to answer." |
| 25–30 s | Ground screen: the doctor's reply arrives. End card: the logo (`docs/brand/logo-mark.svg` on the dark background) with **AstroPulse. Normal, for you.** | "Fixed ranges ask: is this normal? AstroPulse asks: normal for whom?" |

### Recording notes

- Record the live app, not slides. Use **Restore demo data** (`#ground`) right before each take.
- Do the camera shot on a real phone in good light, flash on if the phone allows it. If it fails on camera, use **Simulate readings** and don't mention the camera.
- Everything shown is sample data. Add the line "Sample data. Prototype, not a medical device." small at the bottom of the first and last frames.
- Subtitles in English. Keep the music quiet or leave it out.

## 60-second live pitch (for the room)

1. **Hook (8 s).** "On a Mars mission, a message to Earth takes up to twenty minutes each way. So the astronaut has to be their own first doctor. But every health app compares you with a population."
2. **Moment 1 (12 s).** Judge puts a finger on the phone camera. "That is a real pulse, read through your fingertip. Everything else is typed in or simulated, and labeled."
3. **Moment 2 (20 s).** Replay tab. "Heart rate never leaves green. AstroPulse flagged it on day 11 because it compares you with your own first week. We tested this on a thousand simulated crews: 97% of drifts caught, under 5% false alarms. And our first version? 94% false alarms. We show that, too."
4. **Moment 3 (12 s).** Decision screen, then the ground screen. "When it does go red: what to do now, and a forty-minute round trip that you can watch happen."
5. **Close (8 s).** "NASA lists meaningful thresholds and crew self-monitoring as open questions. Our answer: not what is normal, but what is normal for you."
